Mobility analysis · Las Vegas, NV

The Walk In
Routes to Allegiant Stadium

Anonymized device pings trace how fans actually move from event parking to kickoff — and back out again. This piece isolates trips that begin in a lot and end at the stadium within an hour, strips out everything else as noise, and lets you compare how locals and visitors take different paths in.

Source: Azira Pinnacle pathing & visitation reports · anonymized, device-level GPS

01 — The full picture

Every qualifying trip, every lot

Each line on the map is a real walking route, reconstructed from sequential GPS pings, from a parking lot to a confirmed stadium arrival — no more than 60 minutes after leaving the lot. Anything slower, or that never reached the stadium at all, was dropped as noise before this map was drawn.

Line thickness is weighted by how many devices took a near-identical path, so the boldest lines are the routes most people actually walk — not just the shortest one on paper.

04 — Locals vs. visitors

How locals walk in

Devices are classified by their Common Evening Location — Azira's home-location proxy built from a rolling three-month history. Filtering to locals surfaces whether people who live in the Las Vegas metro favor different, often faster or more familiar, paths than out-of-town visitors.

05 — Locals vs. visitors

How visitors walk in

Now the same lots, filtered to visitors only. Watch the HUD on the right — visitor trips often run a few minutes slower on average, consistent with less familiarity with the fastest walking path from a given lot.

Methodology. Trips are reconstructed from an Azira Pinnacle Pathing X export joined against a Pin report confirming an actual stadium visit. A device only appears here if it pinged inside a selected lot polygon and reached the stadium within the study's 60-minute post-window; devices that entered a lot but never showed a confirmed stadium arrival in that window were treated as noise and excluded. Residency (local vs. visitor) is derived from each device's Common Evening Location relative to the Las Vegas–Henderson–Paradise metro area. Lots with fewer than 38 qualifying devices are flagged low confidence, mirroring Azira's own minimum-device threshold for reliable aggregate shapes. Full field definitions and pipeline logic are in README.md and pathing_pipeline.py.
